# Outreach Emailer - Privacy Policy

Outreach Emailer is a personal, non-commercial tool that sends email from its owner's own Gmail account.

**Data accessed:** the app uses Gmail access only for the account owner to send outreach emails and read replies and bounce notices to those emails.

**Data use:** this data is used only to operate the tool for that one account owner. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising, and no one else can use the app.

**Storage:** access tokens are kept in private encrypted settings and can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
